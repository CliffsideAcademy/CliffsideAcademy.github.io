Welcome to Cliffside Academy

Cliffside Academy: Ultimate Edition is a Roblox roleplay game inspired by the 2006 Rockstar title, "Bully." Players take on the role of either students or faculty within Cliffside Academy, experiencing daily school
life, structured classes, and campus interactions. Players have the choice to join cliques, build their reputation, or fly solo and experience academy life independently as they navigate the social and academic,
environment of Cliffside Academy.




Rules of Cliffside Academy

Respect all members of the community and avoid toxic behavior. Cliffside Academy is meant to be a structured, respectful environment where players are treated as part of a community, not enemies. Spamming, repeated messages, excessive emojis, or intentionally annoying other players is not tolerated. Keep communication clear and appropriate at all times.

Posting inappropriate, harmful, or illegal content is strictly forbidden. Any violation will result in immediate removal from the community with no appeal. Players are expected to follow all guidelines and maintain a respectful atmosphere.

Do not attempt to exploit, meta-game, or gain unfair advantages through external platforms or communication tools. All gameplay and roleplay must remain fair and immersive within the game environment.

Always use the correct channels or systems for their intended purpose, such as using designated areas for commands or roleplay actions. This helps keep the academy organized and immersive for everyone involved.

What is Cliffside Academy?

Cliffside Academy was originally founded during the Vietnam War era as a private institution where parents sent their children to avoid military drafting. Students attended through scholarships or tuition, forming a diverse academic environment.

After the war ended, tragedy struck when a student named Gabriella Dovecote was brutally murdered on campus. Her body was discovered with a stab wound to the neck, and although the culprit was eventually arrested, the incident led to the school’s closure for many years.

Years later, the institution was reopened as a reformatory school for troubled youth. It has remained in that form ever since. The academy is located in Brunswick, Maine, and continues to operate under strict supervision, shaping its reputation and culture to this day.
