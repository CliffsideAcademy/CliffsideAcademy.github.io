Welcome to Cliffside Academy

Cliffside Academy: Ultimate Edition is a Roblox rolepaly game based on the 2006 Rockstar game, "Bully" where players take on the role as either students or faculty within Cliffside Academy, players have the choice to join
cliques or fly solo as experience what a student at Cliffside Academy would've experienced.
