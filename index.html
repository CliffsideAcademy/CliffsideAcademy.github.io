Cliffside Academy: Ultimate Edition

A structured Roblox roleplay experience inspired by “Bully” (2006), centered around academy life, social systems, and immersive character development within a controlled school environment.

Overview

Cliffside Academy: Ultimate Edition is a Roblox roleplay game inspired by the 2006 Rockstar title, "Bully." Players take on the role of either students or faculty within Cliffside Academy, experiencing daily school life, structured classes, and campus interactions.

Players have the choice to join cliques, build their reputation, or fly solo and experience academy life independently as they navigate the social and academic environment of Cliffside Academy.

Lore: What is Cliffside Academy?

During the Vietnam War era, Cliffside Academy originally operated as a private institution where families sent children to avoid military draft. Students attended through scholarships or tuition-based enrollment.

After the war, a student named Gabriella Dovecote was murdered on campus under unknown circumstances. Following the incident, the academy was shut down for several years.

Years later, it reopened as a reformatory institution. Since then, Cliffside Academy has functioned as a controlled environment focused on discipline, structure, and behavioral correction.

The academy is located in Brunswick, Maine.
