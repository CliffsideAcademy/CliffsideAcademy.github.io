Welcome to Cliffside Academy

Cliffside Academy: Ultimate Edition is a Roblox roleplay game inspired by the 2006 Rockstar title, "Bully." Players take on the role of either students or faculty within Cliffside Academy, experiencing daily school life, structured classes, and campus interactions. Players can join cliques, build their reputation, or fly solo while navigating the social and academic environment of Cliffside Academy.

Rules of Cliffside Academy

Respect all members of the community and avoid toxic behavior. Cliffside Academy is meant to be a structured, respectful environment where players are treated as part of a community, not enemies.

Spamming, repeated messages, excessive emojis, or intentionally annoying other players is not tolerated. Keep communication clear and appropriate at all times.

Posting inappropriate, harmful, or illegal content is strictly forbidden. Any violation will result in immediate removal from the community with no appeal.

Do not attempt to exploit, meta-game, or gain unfair advantages. All gameplay must remain fair and immersive.

Always use systems and channels correctly and follow roleplay structure at all times.

What is Cliffside Academy?

Cliffside Academy was originally founded during the Vietnam War era as a private institution where parents sent their children to avoid military drafting. Students attended through scholarships or tuition.

After the war ended, a student named Gabriella Dovecote was murdered on campus. The case led to the school’s closure for many years.

Years later, it reopened as a reformatory school in Brunswick, Maine, where it continues to operate under strict supervision.
