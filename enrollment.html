What is a Clique?

A clique is a student-based social group within Cliffside Academy. Each clique represents a different identity, personality type, or reputation path that students can choose to follow.

Cliques influence roleplay interactions, social standing, and how other students perceive you within the academy. Some cliques are competitive, others are social or independent.

Choosing a clique is optional, but it significantly shapes your experience at Cliffside Academy.
